About

I'm a Data Science MSc graduate (2:1, University of Essex) with a strong foundation in machine learning, deep learning, applied statistics, and SQL. My dissertation, X-Glauco, applied explainable deep learning (EfficientNetB7, Grad-CAM, LIME) to glaucoma detection from retinal imagery, sharpening my research and problem-solving skills.

Since late 2023 I've worked as a research assistant across two labs, co-authoring six papers spanning explainable AI, healthcare analytics, cybersecurity, and software engineering — with work published in venues including IEEE Access and IJITCS.
